Emergency Evacuation Route Simulation & Risk Analysis

A professional mathematical modeling platform engineered to simulate building evacuations during fire emergencies, structural blockages, and hazardous events. Utilizing Dijkstra's Shortest Path Algorithm, Breadth-First Search (BFS), and crowd congestion queuing dynamics, this platform models human egress, isolates high-risk bottlenecks, and recommends alternative safe pathways.

Algorithmic Engine
  • Dijkstra's Algorithm: Optimal shortest path routing to available building exits, excluding impassable segments.
  • BFS Alternative Discovery: Rapid multi-path exploration to find alternative exits when corridors are compromised.
  • Crowd Queuing Model: Capacity-constrained flow simulation calculating bottleneck delay times.
  • Risk Indexing: Real-time categorization into Low, Moderate, and High Bottleneck risks.
